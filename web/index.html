Read this map with care
  • SDAT data lags real-world transfers by weeks to months.
  • Parcels come from an assessment and tax dataset, not a legal survey. Boundaries are approximate.
  • Maryland LLCs need not list their members. Some ownership trails end at a registered agent or a law firm. This map says so rather than guess.
  • The land owner is not always the building owner or the operator (ground leases).
  • Owner types are inferred from the owner's name with keyword rules and can be wrong.
  • Privacy: for homes owned by individuals, this map doesn't show the owner's name or mailing address. Follow the SDAT link for the public record.
